About Me

Biography - A little background

I'm a self-employed web sustainability specialist and responsible digital practitioner with more than 25 years of industry experience, having started my career in 1998. I learned to code at the age of six, and my self-taught career has taken me from software development to inclusive web design and development, and ultimately to web sustainability.

While sustainability is my primary focus, I take a holistic view of responsible digital practice. I see planetary, societal, and technological resilience as interconnected, rather than treating concerns such as accessibility, security, privacy, internationalisation, performance, and sustainability as separate disciplines. My work tends to focus on the front end, but I have a solid understanding of the wider stack and how the different layers and systems underpin the mechanics of the web.

My perspective is also shaped by lived experience. Having invisible disability and health conditions has made me particularly conscious of how climate and societal disruption can disproportionately affect people who are already vulnerable. That makes inclusive, resilient, and sustainable technology more than just a professional interest for me.

I'm a strong believer in contributing to the ecosystem that supports us. Through side projects, writing, and community work, I try to share my time and expertise wherever I can. I've been fortunate to become a W3C Invited Expert, contribute to the Green Software Foundation, and author multiple books.

Ultimately, I'm trying to leave the web in a better state than I found it, and hopefully help others do the same.

Away from technology, I enjoy playing tennis and chess, and watching fantasy films and TV.
